Archival Scope & Historical Significance

This catalog documents thirty-two pivotal comic book issues illuminating key evolution points across Princess Diana’s publication history. Spanning Bronze Age masterworks by Jack C. Harris, Jose Delbo, Gerry Conway, Dan Mishkin, Gene Colan, Don Heck, and Dick Giordano (featuring the origin of Deborah Domaine as Cheetah II, plus Earth-Two Huntress and Black Canary team-ups) to George Pérez's legendary Post-Crisis reboot, Christopher Priest and Brian Bolland's 1990s run, Greg Rucka's celebrated Embassy run with iconic J.G. Jones painted covers, Allan Heinberg and Terry & Rachel Dodson’s post-Infinite Crisis relaunch, and concluding with The New 52 era (Volume 4) featuring Brian Azzarello & Cliff Chiang's mythological saga alongside Meredith & David Finch's God of War arc, Futures End, Bombshells, Harley's Little Black Book, and Adult Coloring Book variants.
